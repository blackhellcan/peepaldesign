From gaps to a plan

GapScan helps scope and price a migration using the 59-component matrix in your original file.

  1. Choose your migration. Major migrations use the original 318-hour baseline. Incremental and custom modes proportionally scale it to the original 145- and 385-hour assumptions.
  2. Refine your scope. Uncheck components you do not use. Edit any per-application estimate to match your project.
  3. Set your team and budget. Adjust applications, rate, team size, and QA buffer. All totals update immediately.
  4. Export your plan. Download the scope, assumptions, and calculated totals as JSON.

This is a planning tool. It does not read remote documentation or perform an AI audit. Component actions describe the supplied Bootstrap 4 → 5 baseline; other frameworks require manual review. Custom checks match common code patterns, and are not exhaustive.
